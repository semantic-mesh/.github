# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Critique ideas with evidence and acknowledge
different experiences. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing and
unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation, taking
the context and severity into account. They should explain moderation decisions
when doing so does not expose private information or cause further harm.

For ordinary moderation questions, contact the initial maintainer
[@azmirabdi](https://github.com/azmirabdi). Do not post sensitive reports in public
issues. Use GitHub's platform reporting mechanism for abuse on GitHub. A dedicated
private project reporting channel and independent appeal contact must be agreed
before community launch; they are not yet established.
